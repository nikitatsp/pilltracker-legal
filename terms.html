Purpose of the app

PillTracker lets you keep a personal list of medicines, plan intake dates and times, and record whether an intake was taken or skipped. It is a tracking tool. It does not provide medical advice, determine the right medicine or dose, or replace instructions from a clinician or the medicine manufacturer.

Your responsibility

Check the medicine, dose, schedule and recorded intake status yourself. If the app's information conflicts with a prescription or professional advice, follow the prescription or seek advice from a qualified professional.
